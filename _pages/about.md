I am a final year CS PhD candidate at the National University of Singapore and Salesforce Research, supervised by Assoc Prof Kan Min-Yen and Asst Prof Shafiq Joty. My research interests include linguistic variation, adversarial robustness, and methods for ethical AI development (both technical and policy); but I've also been fascinated by the puzzle of cognition for the longest time. I'm extremely fortunate to be supported by Singapore's Economic Development Board and Salesforce under the [Industrial Postgraduate Programme](https://www.nrf.gov.sg/rie2025-plan/sustaining-a-strong-research-manpower-base), and split my time between the [Web IR / NLP Group](https://wing.comp.nus.edu.sg) at NUS and Salesforce Research.

Before embarking on my PhD journey, I graduated from the Computer Science and University Scholars Programme at NUS and interned for half a year as a product manager for SAP Conversational AI in the SF Bay Area.

Outside of work, I read widely (my brain loves it, my eyes hate it), especially in philosophy, psychology, and physics. I've also been practising Chinese martial arts for a while now and just started teaching myself the piano!

I am on the lookout for full-time applied research positions for 2022! [My CV is available here.](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ZZ9BEfZTWCSXrzNG2vZ97tyeG_TdKpJ1/view)
